Over time, operating systems tend to slow down due to accumulating background processes, bloatware, heavy visual effects, and outdated components. In this guide, we walk through 7 practical ways to optimize Windows 10 performance without needing third-party cleaner tools.

---

## Key Optimization Steps Covered in the Video

1. **Manage Startup Applications:** Disable non-essential software from auto-launching on boot using Task Manager.
2. **Remove Bloatware & Unused Pre-installed Apps:** Free up background RAM and disk space by uninstalling unnecessary built-in applications.
3. **Optimize Visual Effects:** Adjust advanced system performance settings (shadows, animations, and transitions) for maximum UI responsiveness.
4. **Clean Temporary & Cached Files:** Free up storage drives and avoid file system bottlenecks.
5. **Configure Power Options:** Set your power plan for high performance when plugged into power.
6. **Keep Drivers & Windows Updated:** Ensure security patches and stability updates resolve system-level slowdowns.
7. **Hardware Upgrades (SSD & RAM):** Why switching from a mechanical HDD to an SSD is the single biggest performance leap for older hardware.
